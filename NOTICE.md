## Modifications

This derivative release includes substantial modifications, including:

- Rebranding and release metadata for Update Scanner Reborn.
- Standard, Dynamic, and Popup scanning modes.
- Automatic scanning-mode selection and remembered verification.
- Improved JavaScript-rendered page capture.
- Image readiness checks, lazy-loading passes, and image embedding.
- Improved snapshots, layouts, and change highlighting.
- Manual scan queue prioritization and queue continuation.
- Background-tab scanning and sidebar interaction improvements.
- Scanning activity indicators.
- Configurable desktop notifications and audio alerts.
- Notification dismissal behavior improvements.
